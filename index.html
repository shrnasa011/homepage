A LITTLE ABOUT ME

The short version.

I’m Shreya: a Purdue PhD student who enjoys moving between big theoretical questions and small hands-on experiments. I’ve studied communication over noisy channels, studied property testing in hypergraphs, and spent a few years building knowledge-graph systems at Goldman Sachs.

Outside the formal titles, I’m happiest when I can follow a “what if?” far enough to build something that makes the answer click.
